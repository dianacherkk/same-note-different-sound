# Same note, different sound

## Research question

Why does the same note sound different on a flute and a violin?

The project connects to Chapter 6 of Advanced Mathematics. A Fourier transform breaks a recording into frequencies. I want to see which frequencies the two recordings have in common and how their strengths differ.

## Plan

1. Find openly available recordings of a flute and a violin playing A4.
2. Use Python to estimate the fundamental frequency in each recording and compare it with A4 at 440 Hz.
3. Compare the strength of the harmonics above the fundamental.
4. Present the findings on an HTML page with two figures and short audio clips.
